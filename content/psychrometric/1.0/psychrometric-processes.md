Some of the common psychrometric processes carried out on air are: sensible heating and cooling of air, humidification and dehumidification of air, mixing of various streams of air, or there may be combinations of the various processes.

Illustrating and analyzing the psychrometric properties and psychrometric processes by using the psychrometric chart is very easy, convenient and time saving. In the following sections we will see these processes and some real time example based on the psychrometric chart.
