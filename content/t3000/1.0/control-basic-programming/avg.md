AVG

<table>
  <tr><td>Purpose:</td><td>Returns the average of a series of numbers</td></tr>
</table>

<table>
  <tr><td>Format:</td><td>AVG( expression , expression , ... )</td></tr>
</table>

<table>
  <tr><td>Note:</td><td>A space is needed before and after each comma.</td></tr>
</table>

Example:

10 AVGTEMP = AVG( RMTEMP1 , RMTEMP2 , RMTEMP3 )
