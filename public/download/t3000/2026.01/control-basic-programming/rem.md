---
title: "REM"
order: 1000
---

<table>
  <tr><td>Purpose:</td><td>Add text comments in program to help understand the programming statements.</td></tr>
  <tr><td>Format:</td><td>REM string</td></tr>
</table>

Description: string is a string of characters.

<table>
  <tr><td>Example:</td><td>10 REM *** HEATING AND COOLING SETPOINTS ***</td></tr>
  <tr><td>Note:</td><td>REM commands are ignored by the program, they are only to provide comments in the code.</td></tr>
</table>
