**Product:** MicroPhase AntSDR E310 (AD9363, Micro-USB revision)
**Confidence:** high

Matches Maurice photos:
- Xilinx Zynq XA7Z020 + Analog Devices AD9363
- SMA: TX2, RX2, RX1, TX1 (2T2R)
- Gigabit Ethernet (HanRun)
- Dual **Micro-USB** (USB2.0 + DEBUG) — older revision, not Type-C ES2
- U.FL: TX_LO / RX_LO / EX_CLK; BOOT; GPIO header
